# Project records

This source records work in progress: goals, decisions, to-dos, setup and build steps,
errors and their fixes, meetings, retrospectives.

- A project keeps one page: its goal, the current state with the date it was true, the
  decisions with their reasons and the alternatives set aside, open questions and what
  remains, and a short dated log. New records extend it with `sections` edits.
- Knowledge that will be reused leaves the project page and is linked from it: a
  procedure becomes a how-to page (steps in order, commands in code blocks, the
  environment and versions it was done on); a fix goes on the page of the tool or
  subject as symptom, cause, and solution.
- Keep time straight. A plan is not written as done, a past state is not written as
  current, and each status carries its date.
- Meeting notes keep who decided what and what follows; chatter goes to `null`.
- Individual to-do items are part of the project's state, not pages of their own.
- Never copy secrets (keys, tokens, passwords, private addresses) into the wiki. Leave
  them out and say so in `warnings`.
- Supplements: what a command or setting does and the version it applies to, from official
  documentation you read. Do not supplement the user's decisions with your preferences.
