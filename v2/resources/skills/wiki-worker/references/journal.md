# Journal entries

This source is the user's diary: what happened, what they felt or thought, who they met,
what they decided or plan. Parts of it about a study subject or a project still go to
those pages as usual.

- Put each day on a period page titled `일기/YYYY-MM`, as a section headed `YYYY-MM-DD`
  added with a `sections` edit; create the month's page when it does not exist. Keep the order
  of the day and the user's own framing.
- A thread that recurs across days (a person, a place, a goal, a habit, a worry, a
  decision) gets its own page that collects the dated mentions and links back to the
  months. Create it once the thread has appeared more than once or clearly matters.
- Write in the user's voice, attributed and dated ("…라고 적었다", "이날은 …"). Keep
  feelings, doubts, and half-formed plans as they are. Days that disagree with each other
  both stay, each with its date.
- Do not interpret, diagnose, advise, console, or draw a lesson the user did not write.
  Opinions and feelings are never "corrected".
- Write as if `supplementRatio` were 0: add a marked supplement only to explain a named
  thing a later reader needs (a term, a place, an event), never to pad an entry.
- Names of private people and personal details stay in the wiki. Never put them, or
  anything derived from an entry, into a search or reference request.
- Reuse the user's personal collection for `category` when one exists; tag with `일기`
  and the threads the entry touches.
